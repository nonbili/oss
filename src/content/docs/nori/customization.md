Nori offers comprehensive appearance and layout options so you can tailor the launcher to your personal workflow.

## Themes

Switch between four display modes under **Settings → Appearance → Theme**:

- **System**: Automatically matches your operating system's light or dark mode setting.
- **Light**: Crisp light appearance with high contrast text and icons.
- **Dark**: Dark grey tones designed to reduce eye strain in low-light environments.
- **AMOLED**: True pure black (`#000000`) background that maximizes battery savings on OLED displays.

## Accent Color And Material You

Personalize the primary highlight color used throughout Nori:

- **System Color (Dynamic Wallpaper)**: On Android, Nori supports Material You dynamic theming, extracting tones directly from your device wallpaper.
- **Custom Color Picker**: Select any hue, adjust intensity, or enter an exact hex code. Nori automatically calculates complementary accessible tints for both light and dark backgrounds.

## Typography

Fine-tune reading comfort under **Settings → Appearance → Font**:

- **Font Family**: Switch between **Default** (system sans-serif), **Serif** (classic editorial typeface), and **Monospace** (code style).
- **Font Size**: Increase or decrease text scaling with an instant live preview.

## Layout And Display Options

- **Columns**: Choose between a compact 1-column list or a broader 2-column grid.
- **Bookmark View**: Toggle between **Compact** (quick launcher pills) and **Preview** (rich cards with cover art and descriptions).
- **Site Favicons**: Enable live site favicons to quickly distinguish domains, or disable them to display clean monochrome Nori icons.
