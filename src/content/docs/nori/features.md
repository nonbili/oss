## List Organization

- **Multiple lists**: Segment links into focused categories such as SNS, Work, Reading, News, or AI tools.
- **List management**: Create, rename, hide, or delete lists as your workflow evolves.
- **Drag-to-reorder**: Arrange lists and bookmarks in the exact order you want.
- **Batch move and delete**: Select multiple bookmarks to move between lists or prune outdated items.

## Launching And Browsing

- **Default browser handoff**: Open bookmarks directly in your preferred system browser app.
- **In-app custom tabs**: On Android and iOS, optionally use embedded Custom Tabs or Safari View Controller sheets for quick glances without switching apps.
- **Favicon resolution**: Automatically pulls domain favicons with an option to display the clean Nori icon instead.

## Share Sheet Integration

- **System share receiver**: Send links to Nori directly from the share menu of any web browser, chat app, or reader.
- **Target list selection**: Choose the destination list immediately upon saving.
- **Quick-save mode**: Set a default destination list to save incoming links instantly without a prompt.
- **Automatic page titles**: Fetches the webpage title automatically when adding links.

## Viewing Modes

- **Compact launcher**: Streamlined buttons optimized for one-tap launching and high information density.
- **Preview cards**: Rich visual cards featuring website cover images and description snippets.
- **Layout density**: Choose between 1-column and 2-column layouts to match your screen size and reading style.

## Search Drawer And History

- **Quick search**: Filter across all bookmarks by title, URL, or custom tags in real time.
- **Sorting options**: Sort bookmarks by newest, oldest, name (A–Z), or reverse alphabetical (Z–A).
- **Recently used**: Reopen links you launched recently without hunting through lists.
- **Recently added**: See your newest additions at a glance.

## Import, Export, And Backup

- **Browser HTML import**: Import standard HTML bookmark files exported from Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, or Brave.
- **Plain text export**: Export human-readable lists of titles and URLs for notes and documentation.
- **Full backup and restore**: Save a portable JSON backup of your entire configuration, lists, and bookmarks for safe keeping.

## Cloud Sync

- **Multi-device sync**: Optional cloud synchronization via Nori Sync to keep lists and bookmarks updated across your phone, tablet, desktop, and browser extension.
- **Local-first foundation**: All bookmarks stay cached locally on your device, ensuring complete offline availability even without an active internet connection.
