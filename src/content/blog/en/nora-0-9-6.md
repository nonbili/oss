**Nora 0.9.6 for mobile** brings ad blocking and link and image menus to sites you open from Android home screen shortcuts. The feed reader also gets fixes for selecting text, scrolling, and switching tabs.

## Your Ad Blocking Settings, in Home Screen Shortcuts

On **Android**, sites saved with Nora's **Add to Home screen** action open in a standalone window. Those windows now use Nora's ad blocking settings, including network blocking and rules that hide ad elements on the page.

To try it, open a site in Nora, choose **Add to Home screen** from the page menu, then launch the shortcut from your launcher. Your configured blocking rules apply there too.

## Long-Press Links and Images

Android home screen shortcuts now offer context menus:

- **Long-press a link** to copy its address or open it in a new Nora tab.
- **Long-press an image** to save it, copy its address, or open it in a new Nora tab.

You can keep reading in the standalone window and send a link to Nora when you want to explore it in a tab.

![Nora’s standalone Android shortcut showing Save image, Open image in new tab, and Copy image link actions](/blog/nora-0-9-6/shortcut-image-menu.webp "Long-pressing an image in an Android home screen shortcut opens Nora’s image actions.")

## Easier Reading in the Feed

This release also fixes three interactions in Nora's built-in feed reader:

- **Text selection:** Selecting text in a post description works more reliably.
- **Header animation:** The feed header animates correctly as it collapses and expands.
- **Overlays when switching tabs:** Feed overlays belong to the tab that opened them, keeping them from appearing over another tab.

New to the feed reader? The [Nora 0.9.0 introduction](/blog/nora-0-9-0/) explains sources, timelines, and the media grid.
