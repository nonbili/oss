## After Install

- **Mobile**: Open a YouTube or YouTube Music link in NouTube if your device asks which app to use. On iOS, join the beta through TestFlight.
- **Desktop**: Confirm playback works the way you expect before changing advanced settings.
- **Extension**: Pin the NouTube extension to your browser toolbar, or see [Browser Extension](/noutube/extension/) for details.
- Continue with [Overview](/noutube/) for product context, or [Features](/noutube/features/) for the full capability list.
