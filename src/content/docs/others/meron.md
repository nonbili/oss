Meron is a fast, modern email application that reimagines email management with chat threads and visual kanban boards.

- **Website**: [https://meron.im/](https://meron.im/)
- **Source Code**: [nonbili/meron](https://github.com/nonbili/meron)

## Key Features

- **Chat View**: Read and reply to email threads in a streamlined, bubble-chat style interface without quote clutter.
- **Kanban Board**: Drag and drop messages across status columns (To Do, In Progress, Done) for visual inbox triage.
- **Unified Inbox**: Aggregate multiple email accounts into one coherent inbox with unified search.
- **Media Gallery & Grid**: Quickly browse attachments, images, and documents across email threads.
- **Local-first Security**: Fast local caching and direct IMAP/SMTP connections without third-party intermediary servers.
