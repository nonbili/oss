Nora 0.9.8 adds Pinterest to the services you can open from a new tab. If you use Nora’s Feed to follow Instagram accounts, you can now keep photos and carousels in view while skipping Reels.

## Add Pinterest to Your New Tab

Open **Settings → Services** and turn on **Pinterest**. The shortcut appears on your new tab page, and you can search Pinterest from Nora. Pinterest starts off by default, so your new tab page keeps its current set of shortcuts until you enable it.

With ad blocking enabled, Nora filters out promoted pins as you browse.

## Choose Whether to See Reels in Feed

From Nora’s **Feed**, open **Global feed settings** and turn off **Include Instagram Reels**. Photo and carousel posts stay visible, and Reels stop appearing in your Feed and counting toward your unread total.

Reels are included by default. Feed backups carry your preference when you export and restore them.

## Scroll X and Instagram DMs With Fewer Interruptions

On **X**, videos wait until you stop scrolling to autoplay. Tap a video to play it immediately. Nora also skips repeated posts as more of the home timeline loads, so duplicates won’t move you away from the post you’re reading.

In **Instagram DMs**, pulling down to read older messages no longer triggers a page refresh.

This release also includes updated community translations for Spanish, French, Hungarian, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Traditional Chinese.
